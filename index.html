Quick start

Make a groove in seconds

  1. Enable audio. DM99 creates its engines locally after your gesture.
  2. Tap steps. Select an instrument in the mixer, then build its pattern.
  3. Shape each hit. Velocity, probability, ratchet, nudge, pitch and accents are editable.
  4. Generate ideas. Pattern Lab produces three instant, editable variations without a server.
  5. Make it yours. Swap procedural sounds, import or record samples, and adjust the mixer.
  6. Keep the work. Save locally or export JSON, MIDI and WAV.
